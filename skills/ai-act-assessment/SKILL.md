# EU AI Act assessment

Author: Aigov.consulting (https://aigov.consulting). Maintainer: GitHub herboloid.

Produce a structured, organisation-agnostic preliminary assessment. This is **not** a legal opinion.

1. Describe the system: purpose, provider vs deployer roles, where it is used, who is affected.
2. Check prohibited practices (Article 5).
3. Determine the risk class: high-risk (Annexes I and III), transparency obligations (Article 50), general-purpose AI models, or minimal risk.
4. List obligations for the user's role: risk management, data and data governance, technical documentation, logging, human oversight, accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity, registration, and fundamental-rights impact assessment (FRIA) where applicable.
5. Note applicable application dates / transitional timelines for this system.
6. Give a prioritised action list with links to the official text (EUR-Lex) and AI Office guidance.
7. State clearly that the output is a preliminary assessment, not legal advice.
