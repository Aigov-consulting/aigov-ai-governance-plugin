# DPIA draft

Author: Aigov.consulting (https://aigov.consulting). Maintainer: GitHub herboloid.

Produce a draft DPIA. Final sign-off belongs to the organisation's data protection lead / DPO.

1. Describe the processing: purposes, data categories, data subjects, sources, recipients, transfers outside the EEA, retention.
2. Check the legal basis (GDPR Article 6) and special-category processing (Article 9).
3. Assess necessity and proportionality.
4. List risks to individuals' rights (breach, profiling, discrimination, loss of control). For each, estimate likelihood and severity.
5. Propose measures: minimisation, pseudonymisation, access control, logging, processor contracts, DPIA iteration.
6. Note residual risk and whether prior consultation with a supervisory authority is needed.
7. Mark the document as a draft; the controller decides whether to proceed.
